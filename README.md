# civic pulse
Our project, CIVICPULSE, is a community problem detection platform designed to identify recurring real-life issues. It collects reports about problems such as potholes, water leakage, overflowing garbage and broken streetlights, then analyzes them based on location, category and frequency. By identifying repeated problems and displaying them through a visual dashboard, PatternWatch helps communities recognize patterns and prioritize issues that need further attention. Our goal is to move beyond reporting individual problems and use data to help prevent recurring problems from being overlooked.
